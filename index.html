Dear Soha......

Well... many more happy returns of the day, dear friend. I had the greatest opportunity to meet you yesterday for some time after so long, and I am really happy about it. You are a literal celebrity for my eyes, meri aakhon ki noor hai tu... I really wish we could have spent some more time yesterday, but hopefully we will meet again soon.

I'd be forever in debt for all the loving memories and time you have given me through all these years, and I am glad that I am still in good terms — and indeed more than good terms — with you since 9th grade.

With whatever is going on with you right now, and whatever has to come... as you mentioned before, it's all for your own good, and I really do believe in that. I hope you get enough patience to deal with things, and I hope you take a chill pill whenever needed, 'coz not all things are meant to be held together — if you love something, let it go freely, and if it's meant to be yours, it will definitely come back to you.

Just remember who you really are.... always keep in mind your true potential, and that you have become a really mature and intellectual being through each of the 20 steps you have climbed so far.

Some steps go up and some go down — no matter where they go, just make sure you're climbing them without giving up.

There's a huge staircase ahead of you, and I'll make sure you have my company here and there when the steps get too steep or slippery. Just know that I am here to hold you tight and make you stable again. And I wish there will always be someone for you to rely on when things get heavy on you.

For you, I'd pray a thousand times over and again... stating that you'd be as strong as a lemon tree rooting, that no wind or storm may destroy your fruits of hard work, and may your mind be at calm and peace — for it has to create marvels in the future, and for you to become whoever you aspire to be, be it a fixed role or changing roles whenever you wish to.....

Beyond the field where there's no right doing or wrong doing... I will meet you there.

Your loving friend,
Srutz
